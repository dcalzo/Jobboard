The job board is a service where people
who need to hire professionals can post
the services they need for a project.
Professionals can find these
listings and carry out the work.

Once the work is completed,
the professional moves the task to the review column.
The client checks that everything is working properly.
If it is, the client moves the task to the completed
column and makes the payment. The professional
receives the agreed amount, and the service
platform keeps 5% of the payment.
